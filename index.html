What Are Champion Trees?

Champion trees are the largest known examples of their species within a defined region. In Ohio, the designation follows a standardized measurement system that evaluates trunk circumference, overall height, and average canopy spread. These measurements are combined into a scoring formula to determine champion status.

Unlike folklore or local legend, champion tree recognition is scientific and formally recorded. Forestry professionals, researchers, and citizen naturalists collaborate to identify and document trees with extraordinary dimensions.
